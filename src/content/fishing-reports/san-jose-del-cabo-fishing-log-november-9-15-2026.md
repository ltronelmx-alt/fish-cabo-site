**Outer Gordo gave anglers a shot at large yellowfin tuna during November 9–15, 2026, but even long outings could end without a tuna. At Inner Gordo, striped marlin and sailfish offered a different opportunity over the weekend.**

## Water Conditions and Bait

Water averaged about **84°F**, with readings near **83°F** late in the week. Live sardines remained scarce. Anglers targeting tuna used alternatives including live skipjack, bulito and chiwili, along with squid strips and chunks of skipjack. At Inner Gordo, squid and dead sardines also drew billfish strikes.

## Yellowfin Tuna: The Larger Fish Were at Outer Gordo

Reported yellowfin commonly weighed **40–90 lb**. Several topped **100 lb**, and the largest reported fish weighed about **240 lb**. Outer Gordo accounted for most of the bigger tuna in the regional report, with live skipjack, bulito and chiwili among the baits that produced.

The biggest fish tell only part of the story. Some trips spent hours targeting tuna and returned empty, while local crews reported better opportunities later in the afternoon. Smaller yellowfin also struck jigs and small lures while anglers were looking for bait.

## Wahoo and Dorado: Few Landed

Crews looked for wahoo across the eastern grounds, including Vinorama, San Luis and Iman, as well as closer to Inner Gordo. The source recorded just **two wahoo and one dorado** at its fillet station. Those figures describe what reached that station during the reporting period.

At Inner Gordo, several wahoo were lost while anglers were drifting squid or slow-trolling skipjack on tuna gear without wire leaders. The two landed wahoo took different live baits: one on chiwili and one on small skipjack.

## Billfish and Bottom Fishing

Weekend reports from Inner Gordo included **striped marlin and sailfish hookups** while drifting squid strips and dead sardines. Bottom fishing remained quiet. Anglers working rocky structure reported scattered catches of small grouper, snapper and triggerfish.

## Fishing Outlook

For anglers prioritizing yellowfin size, Outer Gordo had the strongest evidence this week. When the tuna bite slowed, the weekend billfish activity at Inner Gordo gave crews another option to discuss. With live sardines scarce, ask what bait is available before settling on a target for the day.

## Weekly Fishing Highlights

- **Largest reported yellowfin:** about 240 lb. Most of the bigger fish came from Outer Gordo.
- **Billfish:** striped marlin and sailfish hookups were reported at Inner Gordo over the weekend.
- **Water and bait:** readings fell from around 84°F to near 83°F late in the week, and live sardines remained scarce.
