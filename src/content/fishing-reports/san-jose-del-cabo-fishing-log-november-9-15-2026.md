For the week of **November 9–15, 2026**, Outer Gordo stood out for the size of its yellowfin tuna. Inner Gordo offered a different opportunity as striped marlin and sailfish showed up in weekend reports.

## Outer Gordo: Quality Yellowfin on an Uneven Bite

Reported yellowfin commonly weighed around **40–90 lb**, with some topping **100 lb** and the largest reaching about **240 lb**. The bigger fish were concentrated around Outer Gordo. Live skipjack and bulito were among the productive baits there, and some local crews reported better tuna activity later in the afternoon. Several trips targeting tuna came back empty.

## Inner Gordo: Weekend Billfish and Sporadic Wahoo

Striped marlin and sailfish were hooked over the weekend at Inner Gordo while anglers drifted squid or dead sardines. Wahoo were scarce across the grounds covered by the regional report, although anglers lost several hooked fish at Inner Gordo while using tuna tackle. Dorado also appeared only occasionally in the report.

## Cooling Water and Limited Live Bait

Water averaged about **84°F**, with readings near **83°F** later in the week. Live sardines remained hard to find. Bottom fishing was quiet, with scattered reports of small grouper, snapper and triggerfish around rocky structure.

This log summarizes regional observations from the [November 15 Gordo Banks fishing report](https://gordobanks.com/slow-tuna-bite-opportunities-for-big-ones/). Results varied by day and fishing ground.
