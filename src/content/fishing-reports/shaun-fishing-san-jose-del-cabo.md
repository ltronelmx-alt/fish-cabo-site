<h1>Shaun and Alana's Mahi-Mahi Fishing Adventure in San José del Cabo</h1>



<a href="/fishing-reports/category/Fishing%20Report">Fishing Report</a>


<time datetime="2025-10-26">
Oct 26
</time>
Written by <a href="/fishing-reports?author=69113037595a20384a76acde">EXOTIC FISH-CABO</a>





<h2>A Second Day of Mahi-Mahi Fishing During Tournament Season</h2><p>October 2025 was an exciting time for fishing in Los Cabos, with the famous Bisbee tournament bringing additional activity to the region.</p><p>For Shaun and his girlfriend Alana, it was another opportunity to enjoy a private fishing adventure with Exotic Fish Cabo.</p><p>Departing from Puerto Los Cabos Marina in San José del Cabo, the couple headed out for their second fishing trip, hoping to experience more of the region's offshore fishing opportunities.</p><p>With tournament activity increasing the number of boats around popular fishing grounds, finding productive areas away from the crowds became an important part of the day's strategy.</p><p>Rather than concentrating on the busiest locations, the crew explored quieter waters where fishing opportunities remained promising.</p><p>The decision paid off.</p><p>Shaun and Alana encountered exciting <strong>mahi-mahi (dorado)</strong> action, bringing several beautiful fish aboard throughout their adventure.</p><p>Known for their brilliant blue, green, and golden colors, mahi-mahi are among the most recognizable and exciting game fish found in the waters around Los Cabos.</p><p>The couple worked together during the fishing action, enjoying the challenge of landing these energetic fish.</p><p>Between the catches, laughter, and shared excitement, the outing became another memorable experience on the Sea of Cortez.</p><p>Their adventure also highlighted an important aspect of sportfishing: sometimes, exploring less crowded fishing grounds can make a difference when popular areas are experiencing heavy boat traffic.</p><p>For Shaun and Alana, the reward was a successful second fishing trip filled with colorful dorado and memories to take home.</p><p><strong>Trip Highlights</strong></p><ul><li><p><strong>Date:</strong> October 2025</p></li><li><p><strong>Departure:</strong> Puerto Los Cabos Marina, San José del Cabo</p></li><li><p><strong>Anglers:</strong> Shaun and Alana</p></li><li><p><strong>Species caught:</strong> Mahi-mahi (dorado)</p></li><li><p><strong>Catch highlights:</strong> Multiple mahi-mahi landed</p></li><li><p><strong>Experience:</strong> Private fishing charter</p></li><li><p><strong>Fishing strategy:</strong> Exploring less crowded areas during tournament season</p></li></ul><p>Mahi-mahi fishing opportunities depend on water temperature, ocean currents, baitfish activity, and seasonal conditions.</p><p>Popular fishing locations can also experience increased boat traffic during major tournaments, making flexibility an important part of planning a day on the water.</p><p>Every fishing trip is different, and catches are never
