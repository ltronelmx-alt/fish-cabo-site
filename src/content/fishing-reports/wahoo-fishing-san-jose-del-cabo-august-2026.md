The reel began releasing line as the fish accelerated away.</p><p>With assistance from the crew, Julian took the rod and began fighting the fish.</p><p>Wahoo are known for their speed, powerful runs, and aggressive strikes. Their combination of strength and sharp teeth makes them an exciting species to target.</p><p>Julian maintained pressure through the fight and eventually brought the wahoo close enough for the crew to
