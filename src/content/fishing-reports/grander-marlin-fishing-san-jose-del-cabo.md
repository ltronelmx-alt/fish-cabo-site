Written by <a href="/fishing-reports?author=69113037595a20384a76acde">EXOTIC FISH-CABO</a>





<h2>An Unforgettable Marlin Encounter Before Christmas</h2><p>December 2025 brought an extraordinary fishing experience for Suzy and Mike in the waters off San José del Cabo.</p><p>For years, the couple had dreamed of encountering one of the ocean's most impressive game fish: the marlin.</p><p>Joining Exotic Fish Cabo for a private fishing adventure departing from Puerto Los Cabos Marina, they headed offshore hoping to turn that dream into reality.</p><p>What followed was a fishing experience neither would ever forget.</p><p>After several hours of searching the open water and working the fishing lures, the crew spotted a <strong>massive marlin</strong>.</p><p>The excitement intensified when the fish struck.</p><p>Suddenly, Suzy and Mike found themselves facing an extraordinary challenge.</p><p>The powerful marlin fought with incredible determination, making strong runs and spectacular movements as the battle continued.</p><p>For <strong>more than three hours</strong>, the struggle tested their patience, endurance, and concentration.</p><p>With support from the crew, they continued working to bring the enormous fish closer to the boat.</p><p>Unfortunately, the marlin did not survive the prolonged encounter. The exact cause could not be established from the available information.</p><p>It was a difficult ending to an extraordinary experience, reminding everyone aboard of both the power and vulnerability of these magnificent animals.</p><p>For Suzy and Mike, the day became much more than a fishing adventure. It was an unforgettable encounter with one of the ocean's most remarkable predators and a reminder of the importance of respecting marine wildlife.</p><p><strong>Trip Highlights</strong></p><ul><li><p><strong>Date:</strong> December 2025</p></li><li><p><strong>Departure:</strong> Puerto Los Cabos Marina, San José del Cabo</p></li><li><p><strong>Anglers:</strong> Suzy and Mike</p></li><li><p><strong>Species encountered:</strong> Marlin</p></li><li><p><strong>Fight duration:</strong> More than three hours</p></li><li><p><strong>Catch highlight:</strong> An exceptionally large marlin</p></li><li><p><strong>Outcome:</strong> The fish did not survive the encounter</p></li><li><p><strong>Experience:</strong> Private offshore fishing charter</p></li></ul><p>Marlin are among the most sought-after game fish in Los Cabos, known for their impressive size, strength, and spectacular fighting ability.</p><p>Their presence depends on seasonal movements, water temperatures, ocean currents, and baitfish availability.</p><p>Responsible sportfishing practices and proper fish handling are important considerations during encounters with these powerful animals.</p><p>Every fishing trip is different, and catches are never guaranteed.</p><h4>Plan Your Own Fishing Adventure in San José del Cabo</h4><p>Interested in experiencing the excitement of marlin fishing in Los Cabos?</p><p>Explore our <a href="https://fish-cabo.com/fishing-calendar"><strong>Fishing Calendar</strong></a> to discover typical seasonal fishing patterns, or browse our <a href="https://fish-cabo.com/fishing-charters"><strong>Private Fishing Charters</strong></a> to compare 6-hour and 8-hour trips.</p><p>All Exotic Fish Cabo charters depart exclusively from Puerto Los Cabos Marina in San José del Cabo.
