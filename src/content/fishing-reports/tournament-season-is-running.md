<h2>An Exciting Mahi-Mahi Adventure During Cabo Tournament Season</h2><p>October is an exciting time for sportfishing in Los Cabos, with major fishing tournaments bringing extra energy to the region.</p><p>For Amanda, it was the perfect opportunity to enjoy a memorable private fishing adventure with Exotic Fish Cabo.</p><p>Departing from Puerto Los Cabos Marina in San José del Cabo, Amanda headed out early in the morning, eager to experience the fishing opportunities of the Sea of Cortez.</p><p>As the sun rose over the water, the excitement began to build.</p><p>Before long, the crew encountered a school of <strong>mahi-mahi (dorado)</strong>, their brilliant colors flashing beneath the surface.</p><p>Known for their spectacular jumps and energetic fights, mahi-mahi are among the most exciting fish to catch in Los Cabos.</p><p>Amanda soon hooked her first fish, beginning an exciting battle as the dorado resisted.</p><p>With determination and assistance from the crew, she successfully landed her catch.</p><p>But the adventure wasn't over.</p><p>Throughout the morning, Amanda continued catching mahi-mahi, turning an already exciting fishing trip into a memorable experience.</p><p>Her enthusiasm grew with every catch, making the day a wonderful example of what private sportfishing in San José del Cabo can offer.</p><p>Although the region was buzzing with tournament activity, Amanda's adventure was about something simpler: enjoying the ocean, experiencing the excitement of fishing, and creating memories worth sharing.</p><p><strong>Trip Highlights</strong></p><ul><li><p><strong>Date:</strong> October 2025</p></li><li><p><strong>Departure:</strong> Puerto Los Cabos Marina, San José del Cabo</p></li><li><p><strong>Species caught:</strong> Mahi-mahi (dorado)</p></li><li><p><strong>Catch highlights:</strong> Multiple mahi-mahi landed during the morning</p></li><li><p><strong>Experience:</strong> Private fishing charter</p></li><li><p><strong>Season:</strong> October tournament season in Los Cabos</p></li></ul><p>Mahi-mahi are prized for their vivid colors, powerful runs, and impressive aerial displays.</p><p>Their presence depends on changing ocean conditions, water temperatures, bait activity, and seasonal movements.</p><p>Every fishing trip is different, and catches are never
