<h2>An Action-Packed Morning of Mahi-Mahi Fishing</h2><p>November 2025 brought an exciting offshore fishing experience for Josh and Monica in the waters surrounding San José del Cabo.</p><p>Joining Exotic Fish Cabo for a private fishing adventure, the couple departed from Puerto Los Cabos Marina, looking forward to spending a memorable morning on the Sea of Cortez.</p><p>They didn't have to wait long for the excitement to begin.</p><p>The <strong>mahi-mahi (dorado)</strong> were active, turning their fishing trip into a morning filled with action.</p><p>One after another, these colorful fish provided exciting moments as they fought against the lines, displaying the brilliant green, blue, and golden colors that make dorado so recognizable.</p><p>But the highlight of the morning was still to come.</p><p>Josh hooked into an impressive <strong>bull mahi-mahi</strong>, a mature male dorado known for its distinctive forehead and powerful fighting ability.</p><p>The fish put up a memorable battle, testing Josh's determination as he worked to bring it closer to the boat.</p><p>With assistance from the crew, Josh successfully landed his impressive catch, making it the standout moment of their fishing adventure.</p><p>Throughout the excitement, Monica kept the energy high, cheering on the action and sharing in the celebration.</p><p>Between the catches, laughter, and photographs, the couple enjoyed a memorable morning offshore.</p><p>Sometimes, the best fishing experiences aren't simply about catching the biggest fish. They're about enjoying the excitement of the ocean, sharing moments together, and creating stories worth remembering.</p><p>For Josh and Monica, this November fishing adventure was certainly one of those experiences.</p><p><strong>Trip Highlights</strong></p><ul><li><p><strong>Date:</strong> November 2025</p></li><li><p><strong>Departure:</strong> Puerto Los Cabos Marina, San José del Cabo</p></li><li><p><strong>Anglers:</strong> Josh and Monica</p></li><li><p><strong>Species caught:</strong> Mahi-mahi (dorado)</p></li><li><p><strong>Catch highlight:</strong> An impressive bull mahi-mahi landed by Josh</p></li><li><p><strong>Fishing conditions:</strong> Active mahi-mahi bite during the morning</p></li><li><p><strong>Experience:</strong> Private offshore fishing charter</p></li></ul><p>Mahi-mahi are known for their spectacular colors, energetic fights, and occasional aerial displays.</p><p>Their activity around San José del Cabo depends on changing ocean temperatures, currents, baitfish availability, and seasonal conditions.</p><p>Every fishing trip is different, and catches are never guaranteed.</p><h4>Plan Your Own Fishing Adventure in San José del Cabo</h4><p>Inspired by Josh and Monica's exciting mahi-mahi fishing experience?</p><p>Explore our <a href="https://fish-cabo.com/fishing-calendar"><strong>Fishing Calendar</strong></a> to discover typical seasonal fishing patterns, or browse our <a href="https://fish-cabo.com/fishing-charters"><strong>Private Fishing Charters</strong></a> to compare 6-hour and 8-hour trips.</p><p>All Exotic Fish Cabo charters depart exclusively from Puerto Los Cabos Marina in San José del Cabo.
